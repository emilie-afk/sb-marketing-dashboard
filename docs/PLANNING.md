# Marketing Planning

Plan an occasion, assign the work, and have it show up in Task Tracker. The dashboard owns the plan;
Task Tracker owns the assigned tasks.

## Who sees what

- The planning owner can create, edit and publish plans, send tasks and update deadlines, and sees
  drafts.
- Everyone else who is signed in sees published plans that are coming up or running, view only.
- The same rule is enforced on the server for every list, search, calendar view, direct link and write.

## What you can do

- **Marketing Calendar:** a year-at-a-glance view of gift occasions by month, with an agenda view for
  reading them in date order. Opening an occasion shows its linked plans and promotions.
- **Plan an occasion:** brand, dates, objective, channels, audience, message angle, deliverables and
  assignments. Save as a draft, then **Publish & send tasks** after a preview of exactly what each task
  will contain.
- **Delivery status:** each assignment shows whether its task was created, with a link to it in Task
  Tracker. Sends are safe to retry and never create a task twice.
- **Deadlines:** when campaign dates change, the affected deadlines are listed, and the owner chooses
  which to update.
- **Next two quarters:** upcoming occasions and plans on a six-month timeline, with suggested prep
  dates and a "Start preparing now" list.
- **Product categories:** plans can link to occasions, product categories, or both, and both can be
  filtered.

Server settings and database details are in the team's private operator documentation.
